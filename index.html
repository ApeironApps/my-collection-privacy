My Collection — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

My Collection ("the app") is a personal cataloguing tool for tracking books, games, movies, VHS tapes and other media. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

Summary

The app stores your collection only on your device. We do not run a server, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, see, or transmit your collection data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you add to the app — item titles, authors, tags, ratings, notes, photos, and settings — is saved in your phone's local browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). This data:

  • Stays on your phone
  • Is never uploaded to any server
  • Is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings

You can back up your collection manually using the Export Collection button in Settings, which produces a JSON file you choose where to save.

Permissions

The app requests these device permissions, each used only for the stated purpose:

  • Camera — used only when you tap the barcode scanner, to read a book/product barcode. The camera feed is not recorded or transmitted.
  • Photos / Storage — used only when you choose a photo from your gallery to attach to an item. The app only reads the file you pick.
  • Internet — used only for optional features:
    • Looking up a book title/author on Open Library after you scan a barcode
    • Searching cover art on Open Library, iTunes Search, or Wikipedia when you tap "Auto-find cover art"
    These lookups send only the title/author/barcode you entered. They do not include any personal information about you.

Analytics, advertising, tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. We do not know how many people use the app, which features they use, or when they open it.

Third-party services

When you use the barcode scanner or auto-cover-art feature, the app makes a direct HTTPS request from your phone to:

  • openlibrary.org
  • itunes.apple.com
  • en.wikipedia.org

Those services may log the incoming request (as any web service does) per their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will replace this page. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change.
